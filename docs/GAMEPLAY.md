# Gameplay

## Daily Structure
Fatewake uses **one daily episode**, not exactly one decision per day. A typical episode contains roughly 3–8 meaningful interactions and usually one defining decision. Required play should normally fit within 3–5 minutes, while optional exploration can extend the session.

## Choice Design
Choices avoid obvious good/evil labels, hide numerical relationship changes, create plausible tradeoffs, permit delayed consequences, and may allow free-form actions. Players should not know which decisions are narratively important.

## Free-Form Actions
Some situations offer **Do something else...** AI may interpret intent, but it cannot directly modify authoritative state. The deterministic rules engine validates and resolves the action.

## Emergent Identity
Players do not initially select a class. Behavioral patterns accumulate and influence reputation, traits and eventual titles such as The Mediator or The Warden.

## Failure
Avoid routine Game Over states. Failure creates story: settlement destruction can create a refugee branch; exile moves the story; NPC death persists; player-character death may eventually allow succession.

## Global Choices
Certain episodes present decisions to the active population. Aggregate results alter shared world state while individual choices create personal Wakes. The first planned global choice occurs on Day 7.
