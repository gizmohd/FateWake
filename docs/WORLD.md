# World & Lore

## Setting
Fatewake begins in the near future in a recognizable world. Cellular networks fail, internet connectivity disappears, electricity becomes intermittent, emergency broadcasts contradict one another, and infrastructure begins failing. The initiating catastrophe is known as **The Silence**.

## Central Mystery
The player does not initially know what caused The Silence. Competing possibilities may include cyberattack, solar/geophysical event, war, AI/infrastructure failure, government action, an anomalous event, or multiple causes. Evidence should support competing theories rather than prematurely confirm one.

## 6:17
**6:17 AM** is a recurring mystery. On Day 1, attentive players can discover a broadcast timestamped for the following day. On Day 4, an unpowered radio activates at 6:17. Broadcasts warn listeners not to respond to familiar voices outside.

The explanation for 6:17 is intentionally unresolved.

## Season One
**Fatewake: The Silence** follows the collapse of normal life, formation of survivor communities, unexplained transmissions, and mounting evidence that this is not a conventional infrastructure disaster.
