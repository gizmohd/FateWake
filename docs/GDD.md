# Fatewake — Game Design Document

**Status:** Early design / pre-production  
**Tagline:** *Every choice leaves a wake.*

## High Concept
Fatewake is a persistent narrative survival game in a shared evolving world. Player history matters more than conventional XP. Decisions generate consequences called **Wakes**, some immediate and others dormant for long periods.

The north-star reaction is: **Wait... this is happening because of something I did weeks ago.**

## Player Promise
1. Choices are remembered.
2. Consequences are not always immediate or obvious.
3. There is rarely a clearly correct moral choice.
4. Failure changes the story instead of simply ending it.
5. Characters and the world remember the player's history.
6. Global player decisions can alter the shared world.
7. AI personalizes presentation without owning game truth.

## Core Loop
**Event → Decision → Anticipation → Consequence → Share → Return**

A normal day contains one episode, approximately 3–5 minutes of required interaction, with optional exploration and social activity.

## Core Pillars
### Persistent Personal History
Promises, betrayals, rescues, deaths, discoveries, relationships, faction history and unresolved Wakes persist.

### Delayed Consequences
A small choice can become important much later. Players are not told which choices are significant.

### Shared World
Players need not occupy a traditional MMO map. Global decisions influence canonical shared world state while personal consequences remain individualized.

### Emergent Identity
Players do not choose a traditional class. Reputation and identity emerge from behavior.

### Story Through Failure
Loss opens branches: destroyed settlement → refugee; exile → another location; ally death → persistent history.

## Initial Resources
Proposed settlement resources: Food, Water, Medicine, Energy, Security. Proposed community metrics: Population, Morale, Trust, Influence.

## Seasons
Seasons advance the world rather than routinely resetting player history. Season One is **The Silence**.

## MVP Goal
The first milestone is a playable Days 1–7 vertical slice that makes players want to return for Day 8.
