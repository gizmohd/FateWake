# Day 1 Injured Stranger — Visual Continuity v1

Adult man, approximately late 40s to early 50s. Average build. Short graying hair. Ordinary neighborhood/casual clothing: work pants and pale button-front overshirt over a T-shirt.

He has a significant but non-graphic injury affecting his ability to stand/walk; Maya is supporting much of his weight. Early panels should not visually reveal more about the cause than the player knows.

Continuity anchors: graying hair, pale overshirt, work pants, impaired gait. Do not add identifying uniforms, logos or mysterious symbols.
