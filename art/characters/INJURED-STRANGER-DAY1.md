# Day 1 Injured Stranger — Visual Continuity v1

**Status: APPROVED CANONICAL REFERENCE v1**

Identity remains unknown to the player during the opening encounter.

Adult man, approximately late 30s to early 40s. Strong but ordinary adult build, exhausted and impaired rather than heroic. Short messy dark-brown hair with a short dark beard/stubble.

## Approved visual baseline
The approved Injured Stranger v1 reference sheet is authoritative for face, apparent age, hair, beard, build, clothing family, injury presentation and rendering style. Future artwork should use the approved image as an image reference whenever supported.

Day 1 clothing is ordinary worn civilian clothing: dark weathered jacket/hooded outer layer over a pale or muted shirt, dark jeans/work pants and practical footwear. Clothing can be dirty, torn and blood-marked from the injury, but should not imply a uniform, faction or occupation that the player has not learned.

He has a significant injury affecting his ability to stand/walk, and Michelle supports much of his weight during the opening encounter. Approved imagery allows visible blood, abrasions and a head/upper-body injury presentation, but treatment should remain grounded and non-gratuitous. Early artwork must not reveal the injury's cause or hidden identity information.

## Continuity anchors
Approved v1 face; late-30s/early-40s apparent age; messy dark hair; short beard/stubble; dark worn outer layer; exhausted guarded expression; impaired movement.

Do not add identifying uniforms, logos, mysterious symbols or clues not established by canonical state.

The approved visual reference supersedes the earlier late-40s/early-50s, graying-hair description.
