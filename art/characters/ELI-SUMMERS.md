# Eli Summers — Visual Continuity v1

**Status: APPROVED CANONICAL REFERENCE v1**

Role: Michelle Summers' eight-year-old son; curious, bright and highly observant.
Eight-year-old Latino boy. Small/slender child build. Brown eyes and thick, messy/tousled medium-brown hair. He should plausibly resemble Michelle without looking like a miniature copy of her.

## Approved visual baseline
The approved Eli Summers v1 reference sheet is authoritative for face, proportions, hair, body type, family resemblance, rendering style and Day 1 presentation. Future artwork should use the approved image as an image reference whenever supported.

Day 1 baseline is practical layered everyday clothing: dark blue/charcoal hoodie or light jacket over a muted graphic-free or visually subdued shirt, neutral practical pants and ordinary sneakers. A school/day backpack may appear only when canonical scene/loadout state supports it; it is not permanently attached to the character.

Visual personality: frightened when circumstances warrant it, but always watching and processing. His gaze often notices details adults miss. Expressions should retain a believable eight-year-old quality rather than making him prematurely hardened.

## Continuity anchors
Approved v1 face; brown eyes; tousled brown hair; small child silhouette; dark blue/charcoal outer layer; observant gaze; visible family resemblance to Michelle.

Never depict Eli with adult proportions, weapons without explicit canonical justification, sexualized styling, or stylized horror treatment.

The approved visual reference supersedes conflicting pre-approval prose descriptions.
