# Artwork Prompt — day1-michelle-street

## Required context
Apply `art/STYLE-BIBLE.md`.
Apply `art/characters/MICHELLE-SUMMERS.md`, `ELI-SUMMERS.md`, and `INJURED-STRANGER-DAY1.md`.
When available, use approved reference images for all recurring characters and this neighborhood.

## Purpose
Primary interactive panel for Survivor Day 1, approximately 6:17 AM. This is one of the reference images that establishes Fatewake's visual identity.

## Scene
View from just inside the player's darkened suburban home looking outward toward the neighboring property/street in the first minutes of a sudden infrastructure failure. Early morning. Electricity has just failed. The neighborhood is intact and ordinary, not ruined.

Michelle Summers is the visual focus outside, struggling to support the injured stranger whose arm is over her shoulder. She is capable but physically strained. Eli stands several steps behind Michelle near the neighboring doorway, frightened and intensely observant.

A few stopped/parked vehicles and subtle signs of confusion can be visible deeper in the scene. Nothing should look like a conventional apocalypse yet. The unsettling quality comes from the abrupt silence, stopped technology, uncertain people and composition.

Frame the foreground with part of the player's doorway/window so the viewer feels they are deciding whether to cross the threshold. Preserve clear negative space in the lower portion for Fatewake interaction UI and avoid placing critical faces there.

Michelle is looking toward the player/viewer and urgently asking for help, but DO NOT render dialogue text or a speech balloon in the image.

## Composition
9:16 portrait master.
Player POV / over-threshold composition.
Michelle and injured stranger around middle-right safe area; Eli visible behind them.
Foreground interior significantly darker than exterior.
Subtle early morning illumination; restrained desaturated palette.
Faces readable at mobile size.
Strong depth: dark interior foreground → Michelle group → quiet street/background.

## Emotional target
The instant before the player's first meaningful choice: ordinary neighborly obligation colliding with uncertainty and fear.

## Do not include
No zombies, monsters, military occupation, burning city, destroyed houses, gore, supernatural glow, visible anomaly, futuristic vehicles, weapons in Michelle's hands, embedded captions, UI, logo, watermark or speech text.
