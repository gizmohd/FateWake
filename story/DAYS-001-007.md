# Days 1–7 — Vertical Slice Story

## Day 1 — The Silence
At **6:17 AM**, the player's phone shows no service. Power flickers and fails. Emergency radio audio breaks through: *...remain indoors. Do not attempt to—*

Outside, Michelle Summers appears to be struggling with a man at her door while Eli is behind her.

Initial actions: Intervene; call out from safety; stay inside; investigate the radio; or do something else.

The apparent attacker is actually injured and seeking Michelle's medical help. The player's approach forms Michelle's first impression. Attentive players can discover the broadcast timestamp appears to be **6:17 AM tomorrow**. At approximately 6:43 AM, powered devices shut off simultaneously.

Possible Wakes: MAYA_FIRST_IMPRESSION, INJURED_STRANGER, FUTURE_BROADCAST_DISCOVERED.

## Day 2 — Twenty-Four Hours
The opening reflects Day 1. Water pressure is fading and refrigeration is failing. The neighborhood discusses pooling resources.

Core choices: contribute everything honestly; contribute some; hide resources; refuse; or custom action. Relationship numbers remain hidden.

## Day 3 — 11:42 PM
Breaking glass wakes the player. Michael McDermott, a teenager, is stealing food and says his younger sister has not eaten.

The player can let him leave with food, make him return it, detain him, ask to meet his sister, call the others, or use a custom action.

Investigation reveals Michael McDermott's parents appear to have **vanished**. Their belongings remain. Michael McDermott says his father heard somebody outside calling his name.

## Day 4 — The Broadcast
At exactly **6:17 AM**, a radio activates despite lacking power. The broadcast warns listeners to avoid major transportation corridors and says that if they hear familiar voices outside their residence, they should not respond.

Players who found the Day 1 timestamp recognize the transmission. Marcus wants the neighborhood barricaded; Michelle wants to search nearby homes. The player chooses a side or proposes another plan.

## Day 5 — The Pharmacy
Michelle needs insulin for an elderly survivor. The player undertakes the first major expedition and chooses companions.

At the pharmacy another survivor group also needs insulin. Three usable boxes remain; both groups claim to need two. The player may negotiate, take one/two/all, threaten, surrender the supply, or propose a creative solution. There is intentionally no clean answer.

## Day 6 — Someone Is Watching
Someone enters the neighborhood overnight but steals nothing. A handwritten note is left beneath the player's door:

> **STOP LISTENING TO 6:17**

Even players who never investigated the radio receive it. The Journal feature may become available here, preserving system-recorded events and player notes/theories.

## Day 7 — The First Wake
The player wakes at **6:16 AM**. At 6:17, functioning radios activate. A voice asks anyone who can hear it to answer. A second voice interrupts:

> **DO NOT ANSWER.**

The first global choice: Respond; remain silent; attempt to trace; jam the frequency; or custom action.

The global result is not immediately revealed. The player receives a first-week recap with personal outcomes, behavioral observations and selected population comparisons.

The episode ends with an unresolved Wake and the hook: **Tomorrow, the world learns whether humanity answered.**

## Vertical Slice Success Criterion
Days 1–7 succeed if players actively want to return for Day 8—not merely because a notification tells them to.
